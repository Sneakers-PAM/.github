# Governance

Sneakers-PAM is maintained under a lightweight, single-maintainer model while the
project is young. This document will grow as the contributor base does.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** have write access across the org's repositories, set direction and
  roadmap, and make the final call on anything that doesn't reach consensus on its own.
  See [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) for the current list.
- **Contributors** are anyone who opens an issue, a pull request, or participates in a
  discussion. No membership or approval is required to contribute.

## Decision making

Day-to-day decisions (code review, small design choices, bug triage) are made by lazy
consensus: a proposal posted as an issue or pull request is considered accepted if no
maintainer objects within a reasonable review window. Silence is consent.

Larger decisions (breaking changes, new repositories, security-sensitive design, adding
or removing maintainers) need explicit sign-off from a maintainer before merging, even
under lazy consensus.

Disagreements that can't be resolved in review are decided by the maintainers.

## Becoming a maintainer

There's no fixed quota or schedule. A contributor is considered for maintainer status
after a sustained track record of accepted, well-reviewed contributions and sound
judgment in reviewing others' work. An existing maintainer proposes the addition in an
issue; it's accepted by lazy consensus among current maintainers.

A maintainer who steps back should say so in an issue; access is removed once they
confirm, or after a long period of inactivity with no response.

## Changes to this document

Proposed the same way as any other change: an issue or pull request, decided by lazy
consensus among maintainers.
