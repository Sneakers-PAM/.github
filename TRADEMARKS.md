# Trademarks

Sneakers-PAM™ and the Sneakers-PAM logo are unregistered (common-law) trademarks of
the rabbit hole tech, llc. They're not registered yet, which is why you'll see ™ rather than ®. Applications
to register them are being prepared, so full registered protection is pending;
common-law rights already apply from first use.

## What's fine, no permission needed

- Referring to the project, or any of its services, by name in your own writing,
  talks, docs or code.
- Saying your product or integration "works with Sneakers-PAM" or "is based on
  Sneakers-PAM".
- Redistributing the project unmodified, under its own name.

## What needs permission

- Running Sneakers-PAM as a hosted or SaaS offering under the Sneakers-PAM name.
- Using "Sneakers-PAM" (or anything confusingly close to it) as the name of your own
  product or company.
- Distributing a modified fork under the Sneakers-PAM name. A fork needs a name of
  its own.

## Logo

The Sneakers-PAM logo may be used unmodified, and only to refer to the project
itself. It may not be altered, combined with another mark, or used in a way that
implies endorsement.
